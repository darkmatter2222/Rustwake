## How often the order is even in play

A round is **ambiguous** when the store already covers the next unbuilt blueprint's price *and* the credit balance covers at least one machine's upgrade, so the two swapped checks would each claim a different action. The remaining 1800 of the 1800 rounds are decided by exactly one available action, or by the connect and sell stages that sit outside the swap.

## Reading

The two orders finish with the same 8 machines, the same 1757 batch completions (58.6/min), the same 25 credits and the same 3 research nodes, so within this 30-minute window the build/upgrade priority is not the lever. The reason is in the counts above: only 0 of 1800 rounds (0.0 %) have both branches affordable at once, and an upgrade costs credits while a build costs materials, so one of the two is usually the only possible action. The shipped order in `plan.ts` (build before upgrade, so the yard grows its stage count while a young factory is still short of stages) is therefore kept unchanged.
