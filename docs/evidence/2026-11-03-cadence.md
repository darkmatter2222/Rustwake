## How often the order is even in play

A round is **ambiguous** when the store already covers the next unbuilt blueprint's price *and* the credit balance covers at least one machine's upgrade, so the two swapped checks would each claim a different action. The remaining 1704 of the 1800 rounds are decided by exactly one available action, or by the connect and sell stages that sit outside the swap.

## Reading

Both orders complete the full yard: 8 machines, a 5-drone fleet and 3 research nodes in each row, so the build/upgrade priority is not a reachability lever. Under the #214 retuned seed and the shared dismantle-refund recovery it is a mild efficiency lever: build-first finishes 60 batches ahead of upgrade-first (1910 vs 1850, 3.1 %) with 75 vs 145 credits left and levels within one. The divergence is purse timing, not a stall: the recovery plus the seed let both swapped branches open in some rounds (96 and 32 of 1800), which is realistic path-dependence. The shipped order in `plan.ts` (build before upgrade, so the yard grows its stage count while a young factory is still short of stages) is kept unchanged, and the authoritative spec 23.2 gate `validateBehaviour` stays green on it.
