## 1. Ruling (a): v1 is **MET** by `487d3ca`; the fixed 20-tap checkpoint is retired prospectively as the wrong instrument

**Decision: keep (preview acceptance), on v1 as written; the g1 NOT MET stays on the record unchanged.**

- v1, sentence by sentence, on the QA-reproduced tap-20 resting frame: (1) primary `Build Scrap sorter 20 scrap` - one press builds that named machine for that number; (2) toast `Need 20 scrap · have 0` - both numbers, read inside 1.8 s; (3) `.status-line` `Drones 1 · Power 8 · 0 requested · 100 %` with chips `Scrap0 | Credits0 | Store0/900` - fleet, draw, and whether anything has come out. All three matched the sim state read from the same build. **Pass.**
- Retained failure, not smoothed: on the pre-fix merged build (`index-Br0Eyz3s.js`) the words were identical to `24daf0e` at tap 20, the shortfall had no number, the power line existed only from ~tap 95, 45 label mutations in 60 taps on both builds, 0 machines at tap 60 on both. That is the base's state under v1; `487d3ca` is the retest of the same criterion. The label-stability condition is now witnessed independently of the persona: **3 distinct primary strings / 2 transitions in 60 taps** against the base's 4 / 45, and 17 vs 23 distinct with 30 vs 579 transitions at 700 taps. Two-machine separability is likewise witnessed by number: legend `Batch pips · Scrap sorter 5/5 · Furnace 2/5` equalled the save's `inBuf`/`batchPower` at the same instant.
- **Why the checkpoint itself is the wrong instrument**, from this candidate's own numbers: at tap 20 `machines == 0`, so the pip-legend node does not exist and two of the five new surfaces (legend, two-machine separability) are structurally untestable at the checkpoint that decides the slice; the same tap index lands on different ladder phases across builds (the build->place flip sits at taps 21-22 in the candidate's own card dump), so "identical words at tap 20" can be a phase-alignment artifact rather than a product difference; and the pair of numbers that actually separates the builds is a *count* of transitions over a window, not a checkpoint value. Same failure mode I ruled on in `ART-001-decision-g4.md` §3: a fixed coarse sample of a wrapped cycle reports the sampler.
- **PLAY-001 v2, prospective only** (v1's verdict is not re-derived backwards):
  - **C1** = first frame with `machines == 0` and a non-null toast; **C2** = first frame with `machines == 1`; **C3** = first frame with `machines >= 2`. Checkpoints are named by observable state, never by tap count. Pass at a checkpoint = every quantity the persona writes equals the sim field read at the *same frame index* of the same build.
  - Label rule with a named unit: for each **committed ladder step** (the objective's step index, not the authored card sentence) `distinct(primaryLabel) == 1`, and over a 700-tap window `labelTransitions <= stepChanges + 1`. Under this rule the residual is recorded rather than scored: one card sentence (`Refine metal into trade goods · build a furnace`) covers three steps and so three wordings, i.e. the card line changes with the step while the button stays one string.
  - Comparison to its own base, replacing "strictly better at a fixed tap count": the candidate must (i) pass every checkpoint the base passes, (ii) show strictly fewer `labelTransitions` in the same 700-tap window, and (iii) not increase `card.height` beyond CH-1 below. Equal on all three = not better = revert, no partial merge chasing.
- Instrument note for the next receipt: `Build Scrap sorter20 scrap` in a chromium `textContent` dump and `Build Scrap sorter 20 scrap` in jsdom are the same label (the cost is a separate span). Read the census per node and join with a space; a missing space in a dump is a receipt-formatting artifact, not a visible gap.

## 2. The independent challenge I considered, and what it costs the verdict

**Challenge kept:** on a deterministic boot a three-sentence check is string matching, so it certifies *the information is present at rest*, not that a human eye finds it; and at C1/C2 `machines == 0`, so answer (3) is answered by an absence (`Scrap` never stepped) rather than by a production statement. **Ruling:** the verdict is worded "all three answers present at rest on the shipped artifact", not "players understand the opening". ## 3. Ruling (b): 360x640 text-scale-2.0 card at 316.4 px - **not release-blocking; blocks promotion to final polish**, on a fixed non-overlap criterion

The cap already on record is `max-height: 42 %` on `.panel`, resolving against `.hud`'s content box (`src/ui/styles.css:291`, D-6/E-4: 347.8 px at 390x844). 316.4 px on a 640 px viewport is 49.4 % of the viewport and about 27 % over what that same cap's arithmetic yields at that height, so this is a breach of a recorded cap with a named cause (the legend wraps to three boxes), not a new opinion. Because the floor kit held (min tap 44 px, `scrollHeight == innerHeight`, `pageerror` 0 at both viewports and both text scales) and all three v1 answers are present, the breach does not undo the slice: **keep as preview acceptance; final-polish promotion is blocked until CH-1..CH-3 pass.**

- **CH-1** `card.height <= 0.42 * hudContentBox.height` at 360x640 and 390x844, text scale 1.0 and 2.0, one frame per combination; the receipt must also print `hudContentBox.height` so the percentage base is unambiguous (the old 45 %-vs-51.2 % dispute was exactly a base mismatch).
- **CH-2** `.objective` bottom `<= .dock` top `- 8 px` (no overlap) **and** `coversYard` stays inside the pinned 45.0 +/- 2.0 band, same frame.
- **CH-3** the legend names at most 2 machine entries before collapsing (`Batch pips · Scrap sorter #1 2/5 · Furnace #2 2/5 +6`), so the legend costs <= 2 lines at text scale 2.0; the tile index rides inside the same entry, which also fixes the duplicate-blueprint repeat QA noted. No third line is authorised at 360x640.

## 4. Ruling (c): shortfall visible only in the 1800 ms toast window - **acceptable for this slice's check; must become a resting field next**

The timer is `setTimeout(..., 1800)` keyed on the toast text, so the number is on screen for 1.8 s after the failing press, and QA answered (b) inside it. For one press-and-read check that is sufficient, and the causal binding to the press is what makes it answerable at all. It is not sufficient as *the* resting-screen answer "without a tap": a persona that stops to write can return to `toast: null`. Bounded for the next slice: the last rejection becomes one trailing segment on the existing `.status-line` (`last need 20 scrap · have 0`), replacing the phase text when the phase has not changed - no new line at 390x844, <= 1 extra line at 360x640, same string from the same source, and CH-1 is re-measured after it. The toast keeps 1800 ms; nothing forks the wording.

## 5. Ruling (3): the one next outcome experiment - **unequal named-plan prices** (`STRAT-003`, ID for PM assignment)

Chosen over the tile batch clock, the numbered legend, the second wreck/second resource and expedition/defense groundwork. Why: with all three named plans costing the same, the dock is three buttons whose labels differ by one word, which is precisely OWNER-002's "repeated clicks, no meaningful strategy"; a price difference is the smallest change that makes "what I gave up" a fact on the resting screen rather than a claim in a harness. It is player-visible (the price prints on the button), it costs no card chrome at 360x640 (unlike the legend and clock items, which compete with CH-1), and it is falsifiable in one run. The numbered legend per tile is folded into CH-3 as a chrome defect rather than promoted to an experiment; the tile batch clock, the second wreck/second resource and expedition/defense groundwork are **deferred** - the first competes with CH-1's line budget, the latter two are content stacked on an unproven fork.

**Plain-language check, one persona, no census.** Fresh storage, pick one named plan and keep it, 40 taps at 250 ms, stop, then without opening a panel write three sentences: (1) what my plan makes more of; (2) what my plan costs me that the other plan does not; (3) which button I would press if I started over. **Pass =** (1) and (2) name two different quantities readable on the resting screen, and (3) names a different button than the one pressed. Negative control: the same run on the equal-price build must fail sentence (2); if it does not fail, the control is not a control and the check is retired.

## 6. Alternatives, dissent, art-lane gaps, revisit

- **Alternatives considered:** (i) revert the slice to `c2842e1` - unnecessary, every gate is green and the entry is 1,480 B under the ceiling; (ii) grade v1 with the 700-tap census as the primary instrument - rejected as the pass instrument because a transition census certifies the string set, not that the answers are readable when needed; it is kept as the witness; (iii) call the slice final-polish accepted - declined, CH-1 breaches a recorded cap and no human dimension exists.
- **Dissent on record.** Developer position: the one-line status-line field and the legend collapse are both cheap, so do them together - I cap it at CH-1..CH-3 plus the status-line field for one slice, because an unread card is the failure mode. QA position: jsdom is not a viewport, so the 360x640 numbers must come from chromium on the shipped artifact - accepted, and CH-1 requires the content-box height in the receipt. Player-researcher position: wants the idle beat before the price change - held behind the price experiment, since a beat cannot separate equal-priced plans.
- **Art-lane gaps accepted as still open** (not re-run here, none blocking preview): 360x640 mature separability boxes; the three-phone interaction set; human audio listening; real-device p95; human first-feel. Consequence per dimension: the first two block promotion to final polish for the chrome items; listening and p95 stay open as unverified dimensions with no claim attached; human first-feel is the only evidence that can close PL-6 / OWNER-002 and it blocks any claim that strategy or fun is delivered.
- **Revisit triggers.** Reopen this decision if any of: CH-1 still fails after the legend collapse (-> remove the legend rather than resize it, card back to two lines); any of C1/C2/C3 shows a printed number unequal to the sim field at the same frame (-> the resting-screen field mapping is wrong, revert the slice); a second slice ships with only measurement and prose changes (-> STRAT-003 becomes the mandatory next outcome, per anti-churn); a human first-feel run cannot answer sentence (2) on the unequal-price build (-> the fork is decorative and the two-plan collapse in the STRAT-002 g2 addendum executes immediately).
