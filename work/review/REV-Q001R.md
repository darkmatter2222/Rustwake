## 6. Verdict and findings

**Verdict for `8cfc1725d60864e89062cf519098f3fb86cac948`, Q-001r scope: accept.** All four briefed gates (R-11..R-14) pass on independently regenerated evidence; the two jsdom-visible invariants are now pinned by a test; nothing regressed except the quantified, intended increase in contained list travel.

### R-15 Portrait `.resources` column-gap is 1 px with no measured benefit

- **Seen**: `.resources { gap: 2px 1px }` - my computed read is 1.0 px between chips in every portrait case (2 chips per row at 360/390/412, all three scales), while the base `a8fb14a` had 2 px on both axes. The landscape band (which motivated the change) already gets 2 px from its own rule.
- **Harm**: at 360 px the two chip borders read as one seam, which is what R-14 was meant to remove; the row count did not improve (2 per row before and after), so the tightening bought nothing.
- **Change**: `gap: 2px` at the base and keep the 2 px landscape rule.
- **Acceptance**: computed `column-gap >= 2px` at 360x640, 390x844, 412x915 and 844x390 at scales 1/1.5/2, with the measured first-row chip count unchanged (2 portrait, 5 landscape) and `noDocScroll` still 36/36.

### R-16 Landscape at text scale 2 is still a header-only strip (carried from my g4 R-11)

- **Measured on this candidate**: `panelHeight 104 px` and `containedScroll 572 px` (fresh), 529 px (mixed), 462 px (all-known); the frame shows `Research / Close` + `Data 22` only, and all three `Buy` rows report `OUTSIDE` at rest.
- **Change**: inside `@media (max-height: 460px)`, floor the scroller (`min-height: 34dvh`), fold the `Data` lead row into the head line, and cap the dock to one row (its two-row wrap at scale 2 is what eats the column).
- **Acceptance**: at 844x390, scales 1/1.5/2 and all three sets, the body shows >= 1 complete card (title+effect+chip inside the body box), `panelHeight >= 150 px` at s2, and every `Buy` row reports `insideBody: true` after `scrollIntoView`; document still flat in 36/36.

### R-17 One card per screenful at scale 2 is now measurable as list travel

- **Change**: one bounded experiment - clamp `.node__effect` to 2 lines under `.text-large` and show the remainder in the selected card - measured as list travel and flick count against the current numbers; keep the unclamped version and record the null result if the gain is under one flick.
