## Verdict

**REWORK — still an engineering demo at normal phone size, not a polished commercial game.** Technically the build is clean (fast, deterministic, smooth interpolated motion, correct chrome), and the motion witness still passes live. But on every inspected phone frame the world reads as *a dotted grid with floating pale boxes*: no composed space, no silhouette identity, no material light. The owner's "works but unattractive" complaint is confirmed on the fresh captures, and the numeric gates that currently pass do not touch any of the three defects below.

Per-scene, plainly:
- **390x844 rest/fork** (`live--d7af68a-390-ts1-rest.png`, `…-fork.png`): demo. Machines are one rectangle type; the middle ~45 % of the world height is dots.
- **360x640 ts2.0** (`…-360-ts2-fork.png`): the text card (40.8 % of height, four equal-weight lines) *is* the screen; the world shrinks to a 2–6 px mark field beneath it. Readable text, but a form, not a game view.
- **412x915** (`…-412-ts1-fork.png`): same as 390, larger letterbox inside the canvas; ten nearly identical boxes in row 1.
- **desktop 1440x900** (`…-desktop-1440-rest.png`, `…-seed-mature-desktop.png`): the world column occupies ~46 % of width; pills float at the far edges; two disconnected ink islands. Demo-grade letterboxing.
- **mature seeded yard** (`…-seed-mature-390.png`): a soup of same-silhouette boxes with number crutches; identity lives in 2–3 CSS px glyphs.
- **clip f01/f13/f26**: motion is genuinely smooth (26 distinct interpolated positions); the drone is now a body + stubs + pod, not a lone triangle. This is the one area already at "prototype-plus".

## The three most damaging visible defects

**D1 — The scene has no composition; the dot grid is the dominant object.** In `live--d7af68a-390-ts1-rest.png` the yard is three unconnected bands: machine boxes (top), an empty dot field carrying ~45 % of the world height, a wreck strip at the bottom. The bible's foreground/middle/distant structure, perimeter, worn path wreck→drone→dock→first machine are absent; the construction dots are the most numerous mark on screen — the exact "mostly blank grid; unclear focal action" rejection case. On desktop (`…-desktop-1440-rest.png`) the authored column covers ~46 % of width with flat gutters, so the composition never even fills its own canvas.

**D2 — Machines are one silhouette with surface glyphs; identity needs labels.** In `live--d7af68a-seed-mature-390.png` all ten machines share the same rounded-rect + white header bar + footer-pips outline; the *only* differences are stripe glyphs (///, ≡, ⊐⊟⊟) and footer dot colors, 2–3 CSS px at dpr2, plus the printed tile numbers doing the actual identification. Furnace (no chimney), sorter (no rollers/chute), wire mill (no reels/tension arm) — the bible's silhouette list is not delivered; in grayscale at phone zoom the whole cluster collapses into one texture. The stripe-glyph approach recolors the blocks without replacing them, which the bible explicitly rejects.

**D3 — HUD world-hierarchy and flat single-fill materials.** In `live--d7af68a-360-ts2-fork.png` the objective card measures 261.4/640 = 40.8 % (my own probe), four lines at one weight/size, so the text stack outranks the world everywhere it matters, and inside the HUD the four pills, card and dock sit in two luminance tiers with one accent color. Materially, every machine body is one flat fill (`rgb(138,169,196)`) — no highlight/shade facet, no fixed light direction, no contact shadow under any footprint (machines, wreck, drone all float); the amber/purple marks are the only "materials" present. The thick gray route beam under the drone also outweighs the thin cyan route lines, so within the world, hierarchy is accidental.

## One bounded art experiment (highest-value next change)

**E1 "silhouette-first slice" — five parts, real outlines, one light direction.** Hand-compose the opening-slice parts (dock, sorter, furnace, wire mill, wreck/drone polish) per bible §actual-assets: furnace = squat chamber + protruding chimney + 2-step amber core; sorter = frame + 3 visible roller discs + split-chute wedge; wire mill = two reel rings + diagonal tension arm; dock = pad ring + one loading arm; keep footprints/state schemas. Add a global 2-step facet (top-left lighter, bottom-right darker) and a 1-step-dark ellipse contact shadow under every footprint — one light direction across sheet and scene.

- Files: `src/game/renderer/sprites.ts` (per-blueprint draw functions), `src/game/renderer/scene.ts` (shadow/facet pass), regenerate `docs/screenshots/slice-*` + provenance via the existing `scripts/capture-style.mjs` flow.
- How a reviewer measures it: (a) grayscale silhouette sheet at dpr1 — pairwise differing-pixel fraction between every machine footprint box must exceed a recorded baseline (currently the boxes are near-identical); (b) blind naming: two reviewers name furnace/sorter/mill from 390-px crops without labels, target ≥ 4/5 each; (c) luminance bands inside one machine box go 1 → ≥ 3 (facet), shadow ellipses present under ≥ all static footprints; (d) the unchanged witnesses (no-scroll, ≥44 px faces, clip 26/26) must still pass.
- Falsified if: grayscale crops still collapse to one box type; or the facet/shadow pass drops the palette contrast checks below their recorded values (then shading is wrong, not more detailed); or tap geometry shifts > 2 px (footprint discipline broken).
